**87 exercises total.** Practice now follows 39 source Speaking activities, grouped by the textbook task numbers within each Unit. The concise activity titles are authored navigation labels; source references retain the original task and page numbers. Every exercise records a stable ID, page, task, origin, and separate model-answer provenance.

Scope is the ten dedicated IV Speaking sections. Oral discussion prompts elsewhere (Reading, Listening, Language, Communication/Culture, Projects and Reviews) are not included. No claim is made that this is every oral activity in the entire book. Preparation matching, ordering and gap-filling activities are adapted into meaningful spoken questions, sometimes as one complete process rather than separate blank-completion drills. Pair/group demonstrations and reporting are explicitly reframed for a single learner. Source task references preserve the original activity grouping.

`source` means the English question wording is retained from the scanned page; it does not make the answer, translation or hint a quotation. `adapted` means the question is reframed or supplied with context, including removal of a named interlocutor. `authored` is available for newly written follow-ups; none were added merely to reach a quota. All current model answers, Vietnamese questions and keyword hints are authored for this application. Models are illustrative, not official answer keys or prescriptions for personal experience.

Editorial review considered each English question, Vietnamese question, keyword hint, answer and goal together. Pronoun references were made self-contained when necessary. Vietnamese hints use keywords and arrows, not full translations of answers. The book's two-cups-of-water rice example is framed as the pictured routine; actual cooker instructions may differ. Learners may express different reasoned opinions, particularly about rankings and global warming. Automated tests check structure, coverage and prompt exclusions; they do not certify linguistic quality.

The PDF, page scans and OCR artifacts are retained only in the onboarding workspace outside this checkout. The release contains local practice data, not the scanned book or its answer keys.

## Graduated answer support

All 87 exercises have individually authored keyword selections and sentence frames. They were reviewed against the existing illustrative model and speaking goal. Guided answer retains the full model sentence structure while masking 2–6 key words/phrases, each independently revealable. Sentence starters give an optional English outline with individually revealable phrase slots; each filled outline reconstructs the exact model. Meaning only keeps the Vietnamese keyword hint behind a Show meaning hint button, without an English scaffold. The original source questions, translations, hints and models are unchanged by this addition. Automated checks reconstruct each exact model from its blank segments and validate all keyword positions, while editorial review determines whether the selected words and frames are useful.
